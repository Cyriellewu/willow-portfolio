Predicting vehicle chassis failures with graph neural networks

ACML 2025, first author. Python, PyTorch Geometric, scikit-learn.

My final-year project with a vehicle manufacturer, which turned into a paper. Most of the work was figuring out what "failure" should even mean in the data.

  • Reframed a vague "predict failure" request into three supervised tasks linked to maintenance records: emergency-fee existence, cost tier, and subsystem category.
  • Built weighted fault graphs from temporal, spatial, and co-occurrence evidence, with a GCN encoder and Random Forest head, on about 9 million vehicle records.
  • 98.9% fault-category accuracy; high-cost failure precision raised from 60% to 95% under vehicle-wise chronological cross-validation that rules out leakage across vehicles and time.
  • Case study
  • Paper in PMLR
