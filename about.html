About

I build software for problems that are hard to define, not only hard to code.

My work moves between software engineering, applied ML, and product. I care about defining the right target, making behavior testable, and showing where a system's guarantees end.

I am graduating in December 2026 and looking for full-time software engineering and machine learning roles where product systems, data, and model behavior meet.
